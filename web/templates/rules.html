Official Race Rules

All riders must follow these rules during practice, qualifying, heats, finals, and official 12 O'Clock Boyz events.

General Rules

  • Respect all riders, admins, and Race Directors.
  • No cheating, exploiting, fake accounts, or false account links.
  • No intentional crashing, blocking, or dirty riding.
  • Only approved linked riders may enter official races.
  • Race Director decisions are final unless reviewed by ownership.

Qualifying

  • Fastest lap gets first gate pick.
  • Gate order continues from fastest to slowest lap.
  • Qualifying times must be clean and valid.
  • Any invalid lap may be removed by staff.

Heats and Finals

  • Heat and final gate choice goes from first place to last place.
  • Unsafe rejoins may result in penalties.
  • Track cutting may result in warnings, penalties, or disqualification.
  • Repeated dirty riding can result in suspension.

Classes

  • SM85
  • SM125
  • 250F
  • 450

Discord and Account Linking

  • Your Discord and Steam details must match your rider profile.
  • You must sign up on the website and submit your account link request.
  • Staff may approve or reject link requests.
  • False information can result in removal from official events.
