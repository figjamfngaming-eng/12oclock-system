Official Race Rules

All riders must follow these rules during practice, qualifying, heats, and finals.

General Rules

  • No cheating, exploits, or mods that give advantage
  • No intentional crashing or blocking
  • Respect riders, admins, and Race Directors
  • Only approved linked accounts can race

Qualifying

  • Fastest lap gets first gate pick
  • Lap times must be clean
  • Race Director decisions are final

Heats & Finals

  • Gate order = finishing position order
  • 1st picks first → last picks last
  • No unsafe re-entry

Classes

  • 85cc (Supermini)
  • 125cc (Supermini)
  • 250F
  • 450
