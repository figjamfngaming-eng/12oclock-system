League Rules

These are the default rules. Edit this file any time: web/templates/rules.html

1) Identity & Signup

  • All racers must sign in on the website with Discord and set their MX Bikes in‑game name.
  • Steam linking is recommended for verification.
  • Your in‑game name must match exactly, or results will not link to your profile.

2) Race Conduct

  • No intentional ramming, blocking, or track cutting.
  • Rejoin safely. Unsafe rejoins can be penalized.
  • Respect blue flags and faster riders.

3) Results & Proof

  • Race Director posts events in #race-announcements.
  • After races, results are imported from MX Bikes export HTML.
  • Clip review can be required for disputes (use your clip-review channel).

4) Points (AMA Style)

Points are awarded per moto/race using AMA-style table (1st→22nd). Penalties can subtract points.

5) Penalties

  • Warnings, point deductions, disqualifications, and bans can be applied depending on severity.
  • Repeated rule breaks = escalating penalties.
